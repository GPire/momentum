Last updated: 26 September 2026

Momentum is developed by Giorgio Piredda (giorgiopiredda96@gmail.com). By using the app you accept these terms. Data processing is described in the Privacy Policy. The Italian version prevails in case of doubt.

In short. Momentum is a personal finance tool, not a licensed adviser. Free features stay free; paid plans renew automatically, can be cancelled anytime and have a 14-day withdrawal period with a full refund.

1. What Momentum is

An application (web and, when available, for iPhone and Android) that helps track expenses, income, budgets, savings, business trips, investments and, if you enable it, indicative tax data, running on your device. It is not a financial institution, does not hold money, does not execute payments or transfers and does not access your bank account.

2. No professional advice

Estimates, forecasts, automatic categories, market analysis and indicative tax calculations are support tools, not financial, tax or legal advice. For important decisions consult a qualified professional.

3. Your data and backups

Data stays on your device, encrypted. Momentum has no copy: backups are your responsibility (the app offers export and encrypted backup). If you enable a PIN and forget it, the data cannot be recovered without a backup.

4. Plans and prices

  • Free: budget, expenses, calendar, goals, net worth, expense splitting, export.
  • PRO: €3.99 per month or €34.99 per year.
  • PRO Investor: €6.99 per month or €59.99 per year; includes everything in PRO.

Prices include VAT. The up-to-date list of what each plan includes is in the app's Momentum PRO card.

5. Renewal and cancellation

Subscriptions renew automatically at the end of each period at the current price. You can cancel at any time from "Manage subscription" in the Momentum PRO card or by writing to the address above: the plan stays active until the end of the period already paid and then does not renew. If the price changes, we notify you at least 30 days before renewal and you can cancel before it applies.

6. Right of withdrawal

If you are a consumer you may withdraw within 14 days of purchase, without giving a reason, by writing to the address above: you receive a full refund with the same payment method within 14 days and the licence is revoked.

If you change device or reinstall the app, write to us with the new code: you receive a new licence for the remaining period at no cost. Licences may be revoked in case of refund, payment dispute or abuse.

8. Gifts and trials

Gifted or trial licences are valid until the stated date and do not renew: no automatic charge.

9. Purchases in the iPhone and Android apps

Purchases made in the apps go through Apple or Google and also follow their terms; cancellations and refunds are handled from the store settings.

Momentum is evolving and may contain errors. We do our best to keep it running; an outage of the website does not delete data already stored on your device, but may block updates and online features. If a paid service is discontinued, we refund the unused part of the period.
